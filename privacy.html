Privacy Policy – Rayih Gmail MCP

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Rayih Gmail MCP ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, Rayih Bou (rayihbou@gmail.com). It runs entirely on the owner's computer.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API scopes gmail.readonly (read messages, threads, labels and attachments) and gmail.compose (create drafts and send drafts the owner explicitly approves).

How data is used

Gmail data is used only to show the owner his own email, summarize it on his request, download attachments he asks for, and prepare or send emails he explicitly approves. Each send requires confirmation in a native macOS dialog.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's macOS Keychain. No server, database or cloud storage operated by the app exists.
  • Email content is not stored by the app, except attachments the owner explicitly downloads to his own computer.
  • Gmail data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI/ML models.
  • Email content may be processed by the AI assistant the owner chooses to use on his own computer, solely to fulfill his requests.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
